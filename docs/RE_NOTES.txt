What Riftborn borrows from Prince of Persia's reverse-engineered combat
=====================================================================
Source: SDLPoP (github.com/NagyD/SDLPoP, GPL-3), a C port rebuilt from the
disassembly of PoP1 DOS by David Nagy. PoP2 (1993) reuses this sword engine.
We study the DESIGN (timings, AI decision rules). No code, sprites, sounds or
level data are copied; all Riftborn art/audio is generated procedurally.

1. Strike = anticipation -> contact -> follow-through   (seqtbl.c, LABEL(strike))
   PoP ticks at ~12 fps. Strike sequence:
     168 back (wind-up) -> 151 strike_1 -> 152 strike_2 -> 153 strike_3 (contact)
     -> 154 poking -> 155 guy_7 (bumped/recovery) -> 156 -> 157 -> ready
   ~2 frames of wind-up, 1-2 frames of hurtbox, 3-4 frames recovery: recovery is
   LONGER than wind-up. That asymmetry is why PoP hits feel committed/weighty.
   Blocked strike (167 blocked) jumps straight into guy_7 recovery: being
   parried costs the attacker tempo.
   strikeadv / advance carry forward dx(2..6) per frame: the body lunges.

2. Guard AI = distance bands + per-skill probability tables (seg002.c, data.h)
   autocontrol_guard_kid_armed(distance):
     distance < 10 or >= 29  -> guard_advance()   (close the gap / back off)
     10..29                  -> guard_block(), then guard_strike() if not refractory
   guard_block: ONLY reacts when the opponent is in strike_2/strike_3 frames
     (the readable telegraph) with blockprob, or impblockprob right after a block.
   guard_strike: strikeprob normally, restrikeprob right after a successful parry
     (riposte). guard_refrac: cooldown after being hit (refractimer ticks).
   Tables (0-255 per skill 0..11):
     strikeprob   61,100,61,61,61,40,100,220,0,48,32,48
     restrikeprob  0,0,0,5,5,175,16,8,0,255,255,150
     blockprob     0,150,150,200,200,255,200,250,0,255,255,255
     impblockprob  0,61,61,100,100,145,100,250,0,145,255,175
     advprob     255,200,200,200,255,255,200,0,0,255,100,100
     refractimer  16,16,16,16,8,8,8,8,0,8,0,0

How Riftborn uses it
   - Player and enemy attacks use explicit windup/active/recovery phases with the
     PoP ratio, forward lunge during active frames, and parry -> attacker
     recovery + defender riposte window.
   - Enemy duelists use the band logic and probability tables above, indexed by a
     skill derived from depth + soul level, scaled from 12 fps ticks to seconds.
   - Added modern "game feel" layers PoP did not have: hit-stop, directional
     shake, white hit-flash, knockback, sword trails, layered impact SFX.
