- "near 1:1 replication of Prince of Persia 2 inspired graphics, essentially a PS4 graphics update"
- "sound is good, can be more fine tuned"
- "the controls, especially attack and blow should feel like attacks"
- "reverse engineer the native binaries of the games that have been reversed to our advantage —
   understand how those AAA games produce excellent games and incorporate it into Riftborn"
- Earlier vision: PoP 2/3 movement + Diablo dungeons/enemies + StarCraft factions; endless exploration;
  consequences of choices; NPCs with gravity/story; progression unbounded; Skrillex-style modern
  techno/house/dubstep soundtrack.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
- Original game. No copyrighted assets, sprites, sounds, level data, or code copied from any game.
  Study DESIGN only (timings, AI rules, rendering techniques). All art/audio stays procedural.
- Must stay 60 fps on the M1 at 1440x900 window (currently verified 60 fps with --fpslog).
- Level build ("buildDepth") must stay well under 200 ms (currently ~110 ms).
- `--selftest` must keep passing (autopilot through 10 depths + combat-phase contract + duel harness).
- No new external dependencies.

RE FINDINGS ALREADY APPLIED (from SDLPoP seqtbl.c / seg002.c / data.h)
- Strike sequence 168 back -> 151/152/153 strike -> 154 poke -> 155-157 recovery: recovery > windup.
- Blocked strike drops attacker into long recovery; parried defender may riposte (restrikeprob).
- Guard AI: distance bands (<10 or >=29 advance; 10..29 block-then-strike), probability tables
  strikeprob/restrikeprob/blockprob/impblockprob/advprob/refractimer per skill, 12 Hz AI tick.
- Additional AAA feel layers added: hit-stop, directional shake, zoom punch, white flash, knockback,
  sword trails, damage numbers, slow-mo on kill/parry, layered impact SFX, music sidechain.

KNOWN PROBLEMS (from vision review of in-game snapshots /tmp/riftborn_before.png)
1. Characters read as "capsule mannequins": need silhouette detail (flowing pants/robes, cloth
   secondary motion, scarf, cape), better proportions, PoP2's rotoscoped fluidity (more poses/
   in-betweens: run cycle w/ contact/passing/up, turn, landing, ledge grab/climb).
2. No ledge GRAB/HANG/CLIMB — the single most iconic PoP2 mechanic. Also no careful step, no
   crouch, no running jump distinction.
3. Platforms read as thin trim; need thick slab front faces; background vs gameplay layer contrast.
4. Visible chunk/brick variant seams were reported; baked light pools just added (verify).
5. HUD: needs PoP-style health (triangle/flask pips) + enemy health on opposite corner; nameplates
   overlap/clip at screen edges.
6. Enemies spawn overlapping; nameplates collide.
7. Environment lacks PoP2 set dressing: pillars with capitals, gates/portcullis, pressure plates,
   loose floor tiles that crumble, chompers/blades, skeletons, potions (red heal, green grow-max-hp),
   exit door. These are also gameplay.
8. Sound: fine-tune mix (levels/EQ), add footsteps, ledge grunt, sword draw, better kick/snare,
   reverb tail; music should react (combat stinger, low-HP filter).
9. Attack feel: verify inputs feel immediate (input buffering exists), add directional attacks
   (overhead / thrust), guard-break heavy, enemy hit-reaction variety, death animation ragdoll-ish.
